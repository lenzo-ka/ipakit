reference bibliography are retained. The resources are not filtered by
IPAkit's house IPA parser.

IPAkit packaging change: gzip transport only, empty filename and zero
timestamp. Decompressed source bytes are unchanged. These are editable
CSV/BibTeX sources, not compiled replacements. To recover any source file:
  python3 -m gzip -d a-copy-of-the-file.csv.gz
or use ipakit.phoible_source.read_source with an original upstream path.
